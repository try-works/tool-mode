# Active Run V2 — brand review, round 2

**Concept 9** (was 8) · **Craft 7** (7) · **Clarity 7** (6).

The frame now cashes its headline: intent title, lifecycle grid, labelled rails, actions and a recent-runs rail turn the card into a job record. "Ship the launch angle." does more for the concept than any graphic did.

## Three blockers

1. **Three counters, no nouns.** "Running · 2/3", "2 of 3" and "attempt 1 of 3" are indistinguishable to a reader — second call or first retry? Name them ("calls 2 of 3", "attempt 1") and make RESULT read "running", not "pending".
2. **The dome still outranks the data.** Half the card, with nested arcs that encode nothing, while the job facts are 8px labels. Make it proof, not picture: one row per call (tool, ms, state), dome reduced to a state glyph — or one horizon with the three rails as rows.
3. **The dim dashboard is still a stub.** The ghost panel top-right is clipped and 80% hidden, so it reads as an accident; the left column is empty again from y≈820. Stack two ghost runs behind the active card or delete the remnant, and give that column one job.
