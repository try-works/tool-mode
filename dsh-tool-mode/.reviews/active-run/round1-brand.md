# Active Run — brand review, round 1

**Concept 8 · Craft 7 · Clarity 6 — 7/10.**

"A call is a job." is the project's strongest line, and one lit card over a dark field is the right frame — but the card argues "telemetry panel", not "a job". The editorial left and the receipt rows work; dark grounds with one warm accent now feel authored.

## Five fixes

1. **Give the dome a job.** It fills half the card and encodes nothing. Put three rails under the arc — semantic_rank 44 · decision_choose 30 · structured_extract 61, 144ms total — so the fan-out *is* the program; or shrink it 40% and caption it "3 calls".
2. **Make the dim dashboard exist.** Only a clipped "AVERAGE LATENCY / 100MS" stub shows behind the card — a leftover, not depth. Add three ghost runs at 15%, or delete it.
3. **"TRACE 100ms" mislabels duration** and contradicts the footer's "one trace". Use DURATION, add "2 of 3 calls · 38ms" and an attempt marker; nothing on a Running card moves today.
4. **The left column dies below the stats:** empty from y≈640, and 5 / 16 / 3 share no baseline with the card. Align stats to the badge row; add a run log or pull the dome across.
5. **Three accents fight** — orange, green Running, blue "laya · decision" pill. Neutralise the pill, keep model hue in the dome, and centre it: the apex sits ~1px left and the footprint misses the 48px padding.
